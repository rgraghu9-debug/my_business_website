Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about cards, samples, proofs, and shipping.

How far in advance should I order my wedding invitation cards? expand_more
We recommend placing your order 6 to 8 weeks before you plan to distribute them. This provides comfortable time for digital proof approvals, hot-foil embossing, and door courier delivery.
Can I order a physical sample before placing the full bulk order? expand_more
Yes, absolutely! You can order physical sample swatches. Sample costs are fully reimbursed on your bulk order of 100 cards or more.
What happens if my wedding date or venue details change? expand_more
Text and date changes are completely free during the digital proofing phase.
